What is different

A business lease runs on different rules from a home

The residential rulebook does not apply, and a different one does. These are the points we build into every commercial appraisal{{ where }}.

No deposit scheme

Tenancy deposit protection is for residential tenancies of homes (assured tenancies in England, occupation contracts in Wales). A commercial rent deposit is held under a rent deposit deed that sets out the amount, where the money sits, when the landlord may draw on it and when it goes back.

No Renters' Rights Act

The Renters' Rights Act 2025 governs residential tenancies in England, and the Renting Homes (Wales) Act 2016 does the same in Wales. Neither reaches a shop, office or unit let to a business, so there is no periodic tenancy by default, no written statement of terms in that form and no once-a-year rent increase procedure of that kind.

No Right to Rent

Right to Rent checks apply to adults occupying a home. A business occupier is referenced as a business: accounts, trading history, references, and a guarantor or a rent deposit where the covenant is thin.

Security of tenure under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954

By default a business tenant has the right to a new lease when the old one ends. Landlord and tenant can contract out of that right, but only with the statutory warning notice served and the tenant's declaration made before the lease is signed. We put the choice in the heads of terms; your solicitor serves the notice.

MEES for non-domestic property

A non-domestic property has needed an EPC rating of E or better to be let since 1 April 2023, unless an exemption is registered. The government has proposed raising that minimum to C by 2027 and B by 2030. We check the rating, the expiry and the exemption position at the appraisal and say what works, if any, the rating needs.

Business rates and empty-rates relief

The occupier pays the rates once let. An empty property is the owner's liability after the relief period (three months for most property, six for industrial), so a vacant unit is appraised with the rates bill and the letting timetable side by side.

VAT option to tax

Commercial rent is exempt from VAT unless the owner has opted to tax the building, in which case VAT is charged on the rent and on the service charge and other sums the lease treats as rent; a deposit attracts VAT only when it is drawn on. Whether to opt is a decision for you and your accountant; we make sure the lease and the invoices follow it.

Code for Leasing Business Premises 2020

The RICS professional statement that sets out how heads of terms are negotiated and recorded: in writing, in plain terms, covering rent, term, breaks, repairs, rent review, alienation and the 1954 Act position before solicitors are instructed. Our heads of terms follow it.

Our service

From appraisal to rent review, with your solicitor on the lease

  1. Appraisal

    Use class, floor area, lease position, EPC and MEES, rates, service charge, fit-out, access, parking and planning constraints, gathered on the visit and written up by head office with a rent and a letting timetable.

  2. Marketing

    Particulars with plans and photographs, the commercial portals, agency boards where they help, and viewings{{ (" by " ~ contact.first) if not contact.is_hq else "" }}.

  3. Heads of terms and referencing

    Heads of terms drafted to the 2020 Code, the business referenced on its accounts and trading record, and the deposit or guarantor agreed before anyone instructs a solicitor.

  4. The lease

    Drafted and completed by your solicitor on the agreed heads of terms, with the 1954 Act notice and declaration where the lease is to be contracted out. We are not solicitors and do not draft leases.

  5. Rent collection, management and rent reviews

    Quarterly or monthly rent collected and chased, service charge accounts, repairs under the lease, insurance renewals, inspections, and rent reviews and lease renewals when the dates come round.
