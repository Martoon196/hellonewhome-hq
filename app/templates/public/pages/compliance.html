Complaints procedure

  1. Tell us

    Email lettings@hellonewhome.co.uk with "Complaint" in the subject, or write to the office. We acknowledge within three working days.

  2. We investigate

    A senior member of staff not involved in the matter reviews it and replies in writing within 15 working days with our final response.

  3. Independent review

    If you're not satisfied, or eight weeks have passed, you may refer the matter to our redress scheme (details above). Their service is free.
