Straight answers

What people ask before they apply

Is this a franchise?

Not in the legal sense, for the founding cohort. It's a licensed partner agreement: you market under our brand and earn commission; the regulated agency work stays with HQ. No franchise fee, no territory purchase. A fuller franchise model may follow for proven Area Partners who want it.

Do I need a licence?

In England, no: there is no licensing for letting agents, and head office's redress scheme and client money protection cover the business in every area. You must never do Right to Rent checks or hold money; head office does those. In Wales, Introducers who only refer don't need anything; Area Partners doing viewings and inspections will need Rent Smart Wales training and may need a licence; we confirm the position in writing before your first viewing and reimburse the course after your fifth instruction.

Which areas are open?

Swansea is run from head office. Durham, Andover and London are the founding partner areas. Anywhere else in England or Wales is open to a partner who can show us they know it: tell us the towns and postcode districts on the form. London is carved into zones by postcode area as partners join.

How do leads reach me?

Every form on this site takes a postcode and routes the lead to the partner whose territory it's in, with head office as the fallback. Your area page carries your name, your WhatsApp and your patch. Ads for your area point at your page. Head office replies to every lead within one working day; you make contact within four hours of hand-off.

How is commission paid?

Monthly, by bank transfer, with a statement listing every property and the fee received. You invoice us as self-employed. Commission continues for as long as the property stays managed, including after you leave the programme, for instructions you brought.

Can I lose my territory?

Yes, if the activity minimums aren't met for two consecutive months after the proving period. You'd drop back to Introducer and keep your existing commissions. Territories are earned, not sold.

Who is this not for?

Anyone who wants to quote their own fees, sign their own contracts or hold deposits. Anyone who won't knock on a door. Anyone who's not already in the mentoring programme, for the founding cohort at least.
