Is your house an HMO?

In England and Wales, three or more people from more than one household sharing facilities makes a house an HMO. Five or more from two or more households needs a mandatory licence everywhere. On top of that, councils run their own schemes: Swansea's additional licensing covers smaller shared houses in Castle, Uplands, St Thomas and Waterfront from 14 February 2026; County Durham has selective licensing in designated areas; many London boroughs license almost every rental. Article 4 directions in some areas mean converting a family house into an HMO needs planning permission first.
