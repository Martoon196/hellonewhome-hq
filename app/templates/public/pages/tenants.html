Your rights

The rules depend on which country the home is in

Durham, Andover and London homes follow English law. Swansea homes follow Welsh law. Both are mostly in your favour; the details differ.

A periodic tenancy from day one

Since 1 May 2026 every new tenancy in England is a periodic assured tenancy. No fixed term to be locked into, no section 21 "no-fault" eviction (a landlord needs a legal ground and the right notice), no more than a month's rent in advance, and no bidding above the advertised rent.

No fees to you

We can't charge you for viewings, referencing, admin, contracts, renewals or check-out. The only payments are rent, a refundable holding deposit, a refundable security deposit, and the few default charges the Tenant Fees Act allows, like a lost key at cost.

Holding deposit: one week's rent

To reserve a home you pay a holding deposit of no more than one week's rent. It comes off your first rent or deposit when you move in, and it is returned if the landlord withdraws or we can't complete in time.

Security deposit: five weeks, protected

Capped at five weeks' rent, registered with a government-approved scheme within 30 days, certificate to you. Deductions at the end must be agreed or go to the scheme's free dispute service.

A safe home, by law

Gas safety certificate and electrical report in date, smoke alarms on every storey, carbon monoxide alarms by fixed gas or solid-fuel appliances, and a written statement of your tenancy terms at the start. We also check every adult's right to rent before the tenancy starts; it's the law, and it's quick.

Rent rises once a year

A landlord can raise the rent once in any twelve months, by formal notice, and you can challenge it at a tribunal. You give two months' notice to leave.

You're a contract-holder

In Wales you sign an occupation contract, not a tenancy. You must receive a written statement of it within 14 days of moving in. It sets out everything the landlord must do and everything you must do.

No fees to you

We can't charge you for viewings, referencing, admin, contracts, renewals or check-out. The only payments are rent, a refundable holding deposit, a refundable security deposit, and the few default charges the law allows, like a lost key at cost.

Holding deposit: one week's rent

To reserve a home you pay a holding deposit of no more than one week's rent. It comes off your first rent or deposit when you move in, and it is returned if the landlord withdraws or we can't complete within 15 days.

Security deposit, protected

Your deposit is registered with a government-approved scheme within 30 days and you get the certificate. At the end of the contract, deductions must be agreed or go to the scheme's free dispute service.

A safe home, by law

Mains-wired, interlinked smoke alarms on every storey, carbon monoxide alarms where there's a gas or solid-fuel appliance, in-date gas and electrical certificates, and the property fit for human habitation. Every managed home meets this before you move in.

Six months' notice

On a standard periodic contract a landlord must give at least six months' notice for a no-fault possession and can't serve it in the first six months. You give four weeks' notice.

Applying

Get accepted faster

We reference through Vouch or Goodlord. You'll get a link by text or email and the whole thing takes about ten minutes if you have these ready:

  • Photo ID: passport, driving licence or biometric residence permit
  • Proof of your current address
  • Three months' payslips or bank statements, or your student finance letter
  • Your current landlord's or agent's contact details
  • A guarantor's details if your income is under 30 times the monthly rent

Benefits, self-employed, international student, just arrived in the UK? Tell us when you enquire. We have a route for each of those and we'll explain it before you pay anything.

  1. View

    Message us on WhatsApp or book from the listing. Viewings seven days a week.

  2. Reserve

    Pay the holding deposit (one week's rent) and complete referencing within 48 hours.

  3. Sign and move in

    Sign your tenancy (England) or occupation contract (Wales), pay the first month and the deposit, collect your keys and your inventory from your local team.

Already with us

Repairs, rent and moving out

Report a repair

WhatsApp 0751 355 2133 with a photo, any time. Emergencies (no heat, no water, a leak you can't stop, a security problem) are answered 24/7. Everything else is logged and you'll have a tradesperson's appointment within two working days.

Paying rent

Rent is by standing order on the day in your contract. If something's gone wrong with money, message us before the due date. A plan on day one is always better than silence on day ten.

Moving out

Two months' written notice in England, four weeks in Wales. We'll send a check-out guide: cleaning standard, meter readings, keys. The deposit is returned within ten days of agreement.
